## Slide 2: What ISO 27002 provides

An international code of practice for selecting, implementing and managing information security controls.

- **Confidentiality**
  Limit information access to authorized people.
- **Integrity**
  Protect information against improper changes.
- **Availability**
  Keep information and services accessible when needed.

*Source: ISO/IEC 27002:2013, Introduction and Clause 1*

## Slide 3: ISO 27001 and ISO 27002

| ISO 27001: Requirements | ISO 27002: Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Specifies requirements for an information security management system (ISMS). | Provides control guidance that supports the management system. |
| Organizations can seek certification against these requirements. | Organizations use it to develop and improve their own security practices. |

**ISO 27002 itself is guidance and does not provide organizational certification.**

*Sources: ISO/IEC 27002:2013, Introduction and Clause 1; ISO official 27002 overview*

## Slide 6: Control selection starts with risk

1. **01  Understand the business**
   Identify important information, systems and the consequences of losing them.
2. **02  Assess requirements**
   Consider threats, vulnerabilities, business needs and legal or contractual duties.
3. **03  Select controls**
   Choose safeguards that address the risks. Add organization specific controls where needed.
4. **04  Review decisions**
   Document priorities and accepted risks. Reassess controls as the business changes.

*Source: ISO/IEC 27002:2013, Introduction 0.2 through 0.5*

## Slide 8: Firewall rules and communications security

| Control | Name | ABC implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 13.1.1 | Network controls | ABC permits approved traffic and documents the business reason for each firewall rule. |
| 13.1.2 | Network services | ABC specifies security requirements and responsibilities for its network providers. |
| 13.1.3 | Segregation | ABC separates user systems, servers and network management according to risk. |

Change approval and event logging support rule maintenance. These are ABC implementation examples, not prescribed firewall commands.

*Source: ISO/IEC 27002:2013, Controls 13.1.1, 13.1.2, 13.1.3, 12.1.2 and 12.4.1*

## Slide 9: Ownership and rollout

- **Management**
  Approves policy, resources and decisions about risk acceptance.
- **Information owners**
  Identify the information that needs protection and approve appropriate access.
- **IT and security teams**
  Compare current practices with selected controls, assign actions and implement changes.
- **Employees and suppliers**
  Follow the relevant procedures and report concerns through agreed channels.

*Illustrative rollout based on ISO/IEC 27002:2013, Clauses 5, 6 and Introduction 0.3*

## Slide 11: Questions the standard helps answer

**Q. Must ABC use all 114?**

Selection depends on risk and requirements. Some controls may not apply, and additional ones may be necessary.

**Q. Does it guarantee security?**

Controls need implementation, testing and regular review. The guidance cannot eliminate every risk.

**Q. Can ABC certify to 27002?**

Organizational ISMS certification is against ISO 27001. ISO 27002 supplies supporting control guidance.

**For ABC, the value is a consistent basis for security decisions and evidence that safeguards work.**

*Sources: ISO/IEC 27002:2013, Introduction 0.3 and 0.4; ISO official 27002 overview*
